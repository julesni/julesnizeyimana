About me

A bit about
who I am

I'm Jules Nizeyimana, a digital skills and community development practitioner with a background in Information and Communication Technology.

My work focuses on digital literacy, online safety, Internet governance, and community-centred connectivity. I deliver training, workshops, and engagement initiatives that promote digital inclusion, empower youth and women, support marginalized communities, and advance responsible Internet use.
