About

Digital Skills & Community Development Practitioner

I'm a digital skills and community development practitioner with a background in ICT. My work focuses on digital literacy, online safety, Internet governance, and community-centred connectivity, delivering training, workshops, and engagement initiatives that promote digital inclusion, empower youth and women, marginalized communities, and advance responsible Internet use.
